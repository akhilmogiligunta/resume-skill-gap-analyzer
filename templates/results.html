How to Interpret These Results

Required Skill Coverage shows how many recognized skills required by the job description are present in the resume.

TF-IDF Cosine Similarity measures similarity between the overall resume text and job-description text.

These are separate measurements and do not represent hiring probability, selection probability, or hiring success.
